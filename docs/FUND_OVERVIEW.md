Fewture Fund Overview
The creator economy has experienced explosive growth, but the true frontier is the attention economy—a $1.5 trillion market where engagement is the most valuable currency. Fewture Fund was established to provide the infrastructure, resources, and capital that help creators turn their attention into ownership. Our mission is to empower creators to become founders, diversify their revenue streams, and redefine what ownership looks like in the digital age.
Fewture Fund operates as both a venture studio and an early-stage venture capital firm, positioned at the intersection of culture, media, and technology. By incubating and investing in creator-led businesses, we are building the next generation of high-growth companies with global cultural impact.
Our thesis is clear: creators are not only entertainers—they are entrepreneurs, brand builders, and cultural leaders. Already, more than two-thirds of consumers say they prefer creator-founded products over traditional alternatives. The world’s largest creators have proven they can transform influence into billion-dollar ventures, and Fewture Fund is here to systematically back the next wave of companies and intellectual property.
Investment Focus
Fewture Fund invests across three core verticals:
High-Impact Content & Live IP (40%) Building original franchises, event formats, and live IP experiences that resonate with global audiences.
Creator-Led Consumer Products (30%) Partnering with creators to launch design-driven consumer brands in categories ready for disruption.
Proprietary Technology (20%) Developing tools and platforms that power creator-led ventures, scale communities, and unlock new forms of monetization.
Together, these verticals form a pipeline of opportunities that maximize both cultural relevance and financial return.

From launching Beats by Dre, to scaling FaZe Clan into a billion-dollar publicly traded company, to operating major media networks like VICE and Univision, our founding partners bring the rare combination of cultural fluency and venture execution.
This experience positions Fewture Fund uniquely: we understand creators, we know how to turn attention into sustainable businesses, and we’ve built the infrastructure to repeat this process at scale.
By bringing together culture, capital, and operational excellence, Fewture Fund is charting the future of the attention economy—where creators don’t just participate, they own.
